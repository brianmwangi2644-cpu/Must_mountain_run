About MUST Mountain Run

Where passion, endurance, and nature meet.

Our Story

The MUST Mountain Run is an annual event organized by Meru University of Science and Technology (MUST), designed to bring together students, athletes, and outdoor enthusiasts to celebrate fitness, nature, and community spirit. The event features breathtaking trails across the scenic Meru landscapes, offering runners a chance to experience both physical challenge and natural beauty.

What began as a small university initiative has now grown into a regional event attracting participants from across Kenya and beyond. The run promotes healthy living, environmental awareness, and teamwork while showcasing the vibrant culture and hospitality of the Meru community.

Beautiful Trails

Experience the lush greenery, fresh mountain air, and panoramic views as you conquer the challenging terrain.

Awards & Recognition

Top performers are celebrated for their dedication and endurance with trophies, medals, and special prizes.

Community & Unity

The event brings together people from all walks of life — promoting unity, sportsmanship, and positive energy.

Whether you’re a professional athlete or a nature lover, the MUST Mountain Run invites you to be part of something inspiring — a movement that combines adventure, wellness, and sustainability.
